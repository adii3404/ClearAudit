ABOUT THIS PROJECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ClearAudit is a modern, monochrome B2B corporate expense reimbursement and
auditing platform. It empowers employees to submit expense claims digitally
— via camera capture or file upload — and gives administrators a streamlined
audit queue to review, approve, reject, or revoke claims with full traceability.

The platform is built entirely on a client-side React 19 stack with Vite 8,
Tailwind CSS v4, and browser localStorage for persistence. No backend server
is required for the core workflow, making it ideal for rapid prototyping,
demos, and internal tooling pilots.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Monochrome-first: A clean black-and-white aesthetic that prioritizes
  readability and reduces visual clutter in enterprise environments.
- Zero-friction onboarding: Default credentials baked in for instant access.
- Audit-first architecture: Every claim gets a unique token (e.g., CA-XXXX-XXXX)
  and follows a deterministic state machine through its lifecycle.
- Indian compliance ready: GST-compliant voucher generation with CGST/SGST/IGST
  breakdowns, downloadable as PNG or printable directly from the browser.

SUPPORTED EXPENSE CATEGORIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The platform supports the following reimbursement categories out of the box:
  - Travel & Conveyance
  - Meals & Per Diem
  - Office Supplies
  - Client Entertainment
  - Telecommunications
  - Miscellaneous / Other

Each category maps to internal accounting codes and is surfaced in both the
employee submission form and the admin audit queue filters.
